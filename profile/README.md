## Customer Journey Modeling Language (CJML)

- Services are often delivered by multiple organizations and actors, making it difficult to understand the complete journey from an end-user perspective.
- CJML helps bring these fragmented journeys together, whether the end user is a customer, patient, citizen, employee, or other service user.
- It supports the modeling of both planned and actual journeys, including interactions across service providers and the experiences of individual end users.
- Developed at SINTEF Digital, CJML provides concepts, methods, and tools for understanding how people experience services over time.
